Summary

  • No tracking. This website sets no cookies, runs no JavaScript, has no analytics or ads, and loads nothing from other domains.
  • Short, masked logs. The web server logs each request with a shortened IP address. Log files are deleted after 30 days.
  • An anonymous demo. The live demo uses one shared, read-only account and a single sign-in cookie. It records nothing that identifies you.
  • Nothing sold or shared. We do not sell personal data, build profiles or share data for advertising.
  • Your rights. You can ask what we hold, have it corrected or erased, and complain. Email hello@nullwake.si.

Who is responsible

Nullwake is run by Akhil Pettem, an individual based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. He decides why and how the data described here is processed, so he is the controller under the EU and UK GDPR and the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

For any privacy question, request or grievance, write to hello@nullwake.si. Akhil handles these himself and acts as the contact for grievances under the DPDP Act.

Legal basis

  • GDPR: the masked logs and the demo rely on our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Article 6(1)(f)). Replying to your email relies on the steps you asked us to take (Article 6(1)(b)) or our legitimate interest in answering.
  • DPDP Act: when you email us, you give us that data voluntarily for us to reply, which the Act treats as a legitimate use (Section 7(a)). We use it for nothing else. For the masked logs, we process only what is needed to keep the service running and secure. If you want something we hold removed, ask and we will remove it.

The website and demo are not directed at children. We do not knowingly process personal data of anyone under 18. If you think we hold such data, tell us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, and a copy;
  • have it corrected, completed or updated;
  • have it erased;
  • object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent where we rely on it;
  • under the DPDP Act, nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to;
  • complain to a regulator: the Data Protection Board of India, or your local data protection authority in the EU or UK. Under the DPDP Act, write to us first so we can resolve the grievance.

Email hello@nullwake.si with your request. We reply within 30 days. Because the site and demo logs hold only shortened IP addresses, we usually cannot link a log entry to you, but we will tell you what we can find.

Security and breaches

Pages are served only over HTTPS. The application runs in locked-down containers, and its database is reachable only from inside the server. The details are on the security page. If a breach affected your personal data, we would tell you and the authorities that the law requires, without undue delay.

Health information and HIPAA

This website and the demo do not collect health information. Please do not send any by email. Nullwake, the project, is not a HIPAA covered entity or business associate, and holds no certification.

If you run Nullwake on systems that hold protected health information, it runs inside your environment and reads only counts and aggregates, so no records reach us. See GDPR, DPDP Act and HIPAA on the security page for how to assess it.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when what we collect changes, and change the date at the top. This notice explains our practices in plain language. It is not legal advice for your own use of Nullwake.
